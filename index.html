What is Vigna Misteriosa?

A vineyard spread across the rooftops of Porta Romana.

An urban, distributed vineyard on the rooftops of Teatro Franco Parenti, Milan's contemporary performing arts theatre. It will hold over 300 high-tech pots, monitored in cooperation with Politecnico di Torino. Sensor networks measure the vineyard's environmental impact, and dashboards collect its agronomic and environmental data.

Cultivating culture

Three roots, one vineyard.

Culture

A vineyard in the heart of Milan adds a new layer to a multidimensional cultural venue, where urban and rural Italy meet. Teatro Franco Parenti becomes a place for entertainment, Italian heritage and urban environmental innovation.

Environmental sustainability

A real-world testing ground, with IoT sensors giving measurable evidence of environmental impact. It adds green space in the heart of Porta Romana, and an annual ESG report is included in the sustainability report.

Community engagement

Supporters connect with each other and with the mission, through the crowdfunding and through the project's impact on the area. It brings people to the neighbourhood and gives them a reason to spend time there.

Questions

Good to know.

What exactly am I funding?

The first 107 vine pots of the vineyard, called Section A. Each pot costs €300, so the goal is €32,100. The full vineyard will have over 300 pots.

What if I can't give €300?

Every amount counts. Gifts from €10 are pooled toward the same vine pots, and each level comes with its own thank-you.

What do I receive?

It depends on your level: from a digital thank-you and newsletter at €10, to your name on a vine pot at €300, to a guided visit and an invitation for two to the opening at €1,000. The full list is in the gift section.

Where is the vineyard?

On the rooftops of Teatro Franco Parenti and Bagni Misteriosi, in the Porta Romana neighbourhood of Milan.

Who is behind the project?

Teatro Franco Parenti, Bagni Misteriosi and Citiculture, with Politecnico di Torino monitoring the vineyard through its sensor network.

How can I visit?

Come to the open afternoon in June at Bagni Misteriosi. Entry is free, and volunteers will show you around and explain the project.
